Why Nassau & Suffolk Homeowners Call for Lock Service

Long Island's housing stock ranges from postwar Levittown-era homes to newer builds in Huntington and Smithtown, and older hardware is the most common reason we get called out — worn deadbolts, loose strike plates, and doors that were rekeyed so many times the pins barely catch anymore.

A move-in is the other big trigger. If you just bought or rented in Massapequa or anywhere nearby, you have no idea how many spare keys are floating around from past owners, contractors, or tenants — and that's a real security gap until it's fixed.

How We Install and Rekey Locks the Right Way

We start by checking the whole entry point — deadbolt, strike plate, door frame, and hinges — since a brand-new lock on a weak frame doesn't actually add much security. Sliding doors and windows get the same look, since they're often the weakest point in a house.

For smart locks, we handle the mounting, wiring where needed, and app or keypad setup so it works correctly the first time — not a half-finished install you have to troubleshoot yourself later.

Residential Lock Pricing & How Long Your Locks Last

Standard deadbolt installation or replacement typically runs $120–$300 per door depending on hardware grade, with smart locks running higher for the unit itself. A quality deadbolt properly installed should easily last 15–20 years with no more than an occasional rekey.

Rekeying instead of replacing is usually the cheaper option if your existing hardware is in good shape — we'll always tell you honestly which one makes more sense for your situation.

What Happens If You Put Off Fixing a Weak Lock

A deadbolt that doesn't fully engage, or a strike plate held on with short screws, is one of the most common ways homes across Long Island get broken into — it doesn't take a sophisticated burglar, just a stiff kick.

Waiting also means more people quietly holding keys to your home the longer you go without rekeying after a move-in, breakup, or roommate change. It's a cheap fix now versus a much more expensive lesson later.

Common Questions About Residential Locksmith on Long Island

Should I rekey or replace my locks after moving in?

If your current deadbolts are in good condition, rekeying is usually enough and costs less than full replacement. We'll inspect the hardware on-site and give you an honest recommendation either way.

Do you install smart locks?

Yes — we install and configure most major smart lock brands, including keypad and app-connected models, and make sure it's fully working before we leave.
