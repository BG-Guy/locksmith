Emergency Lockout

Locked out of your home, car, or business? We prioritize lockout calls and dispatch the nearest available technician immediately.

02 — Residential

Residential Locksmith

From a single deadbolt to a whole-house rekey, we help homeowners across Long Island secure every entry point.

  • Lock installation and replacement
  • Smart lock setup and configuration
  • Sliding door and window lock hardware
03 — Commercial

Commercial Locksmith

We work with property managers and business owners to keep facilities secure and compliant.

  • Master key systems and re-keying schedules
  • Panic bars and exit device installation
  • Access control and electronic lock integration
04 — Automotive

Automotive Locksmith

On-site car key and ignition service for most makes and models — no tow truck required.

  • Car key replacement, including transponder keys
  • Key fob programming
  • Ignition repair and lock cylinder replacement
05 — Rekeying

Lock Rekeying

Rekeying swaps the internal pins in your existing lock so old keys stop working — faster and cheaper than full replacement.

  • Ideal after a move-in, lost key, or roommate change
  • Most rekeys completed in under an hour
  • Works with most standard residential hardware
06 — High-Security

High-Security Lock Installation

Upgrade to pick- and bump-resistant hardware for the entry points that matter most.

  • Reinforced deadbolts and strike plates
  • Restricted keyway systems
  • Residential and commercial safes
