## Needs your decision
1. **Home, About, and Contact pages have no `<link rel="canonical">`.** `docs/index.html`, `docs/about.html`, and `docs/contact.html` are hand-written (only their header/footer/service-grid/town-chips blocks are regenerated by `build/build.js`) and never had a canonical tag added. Every generated service/town page gets one automatically from `build/layout.js`'s `page()` template, but these three don't go through it. Google is currently inferring the canonical for `/` on its own (it matches), but that's not guaranteed to hold. **Exact fix:** add a self-referencing canonical to each `<head>`, e.g. in `docs/index.html`: `<link rel="canonical" href="https://havenlocksmith.com/">`; `https://havenlocksmith.com/about.html` and `.../contact.html` for the other two.
2. **Home page meta description is 166 characters** — over the 140-160 target. Current: "Haven Locksmith provides emergency lockout, residential, commercial, and automotive locksmith services across Long Island, NY. Licensed, insured, and dispatched 24/7." **Suggested fix** (152 chars): "Emergency lockout, residential, commercial & automotive locksmith services across Long Island, NY. Licensed, insured, dispatched 24/7."
3. **Home page has no JSON-LD structured data.** Every generated service/town page carries a LocalBusiness block via `build/seo.js`; `index.html`, `about.html`, and `contact.html` carry none. **Exact fix:** add the same LocalBusiness JSON-LD (business name, phone, address/service area, `@type: "Locksmith"`) to each hand page's `<head>`.
4. **Why I didn't just fix 1–3:** `seo-agent.config.json`'s `content_sources` only lists `build/*.js` files, and `protected_paths` lists `docs/` wholesale — but the actual fix lives in `docs/index.html`, `about.html`, and `contact.html` themselves, since their `<head>` is hand-typed with no build-time injection point. That's a real gap between the config and how the project is built (README confirms these three pages are partly hand-edited). Two ways to close it, your call: (a) add `index.html`, `about.html`, `contact.html` to `content_sources` so a future cycle can edit them directly, or (b) extend `build/layout.js` + `build/build.js` so the hand pages' canonical and JSON-LD are injected the same way the header/footer already are, keeping `docs/` fully generated. Once either is in place I can apply fixes 1–3 next cycle.
5. **No sitemap registered in Search Console.** The GSC snapshot's `index_health.sitemaps` list came back empty, even though `docs/sitemap.xml` exists, is correctly built, and is referenced from `docs/robots.txt` (`Sitemap: https://havenlocksmith.com/sitemap.xml`). This looks like the sitemap was never submitted in Search Console, which would also explain the complete lack of impressions. **Fix (manual, outside this agent's scope):** in Search Console, under Sitemaps, submit `https://havenlocksmith.com/sitemap.xml`.
