Why Long Island Businesses Need a Commercial Locksmith

Employee turnover is the number one reason businesses in Bay Shore, Patchogue, and Islip call us. Every former employee with an un-returned key or an active access code is a security gap, and most business owners don't realize how many keys are actually out there until they add them up.

Fire code compliance is the other driver — older commercial buildings across Long Island often have exit hardware that no longer meets current panic bar and egress requirements, which becomes a real liability during an inspection.

How We Handle Master Keying & Access Control

We design master key systems around who actually needs access to what — front-of-house staff, management, maintenance, and after-hours cleaning crews all get different levels without juggling a dozen physical keys.

For access control, we integrate keypad and card-based systems with your existing doors, and we schedule installs around your business hours so it doesn't interrupt operations — most commercial jobs are done overnight or on weekends.

Commercial Locksmith Pricing on Long Island

A full master key system typically runs $400–$1,500+ depending on the number of doors and access tiers involved. Panic bar and exit device installs are usually priced per door, and we'll always provide a written quote before starting.

A properly maintained commercial system holds up for years, but we recommend a rekey any time there's a change in ownership, management, or a significant staff departure.

The Real Cost of Delaying Commercial Security Upgrades

An outdated key system doesn't just risk break-ins — it's a liability issue if an incident happens and you can't account for who had access. Failed exit hardware can also mean a failed fire inspection, which can shut down operations until it's fixed.

Businesses that put this off almost always pay more later: emergency after-hours service costs more than a scheduled upgrade, and insurance claims get complicated fast when access control wasn't properly documented.
