About YZLocksmith

Local locksmiths, built for how Long Island actually calls for help.

We started YZLocksmith to fix a simple problem: locksmith dispatchers who quote one price and charge another. We do the opposite.

Our Story

Straightforward locksmith work, no surprises

YZLocksmith is a Long Island-based locksmith company serving homeowners, businesses, and drivers across Nassau and Suffolk County. Every technician on our team is licensed, insured, and background-checked before they're ever sent to a job.

We quote a flat rate before any work begins, and we stick to it — no inflated "trip charges" once a technician is already at your door. Whether it's a 2 a.m. lockout or a scheduled commercial rekey, the price you hear on the phone is the price you pay.

Our goal is simple: be the locksmith people trust enough to save the number in their phone.
